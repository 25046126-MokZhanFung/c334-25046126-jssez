02 / A practical sequence

The SETUP framework.

Use the Playbook’s five stages to plan a move from early assessment to operations.

  1. S

    Select your zone strategically

    Compare each zone’s targeted activities with your business needs.

  2. E

    Evaluate incentives and requirements

    Review MIDA’s JS-SEZ packages, facilitation through IMFC-J and IRDA, and Enterprise Singapore support schemes.

  3. T

    Take the right legal steps

    Choose a business structure, register, check licences, set up tax and employee contributions, and plan for customs compliance.

  4. U

    Utilise bilateral support

    Explore regional banking support, Green Lane account opening, and market-entry help from SMF and FMM.

  5. P

    Prepare your talent and supply chain

    Plan skilled-worker mobility, workforce training and local supply-chain partnerships.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, pp. 6–7.

Watch-out / Cross-border work

Mobility does not remove tax or immigration duties.

Workdays in Johor may trigger Malaysian personal-tax obligations and extra reporting or withholding in either jurisdiction. Treaty relief depends on conditions. Track where employees work, what they do and how long they stay. Keep records and check pass requirements for each activity.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, p. 16.

04 / Plan for your people

Passes for staff.

The Appendix outlines expatriate posts and temporary professional visits. Requirements depend on the role and activity.

MIDA expatriate posts

Key Post or Term Post

Key Post: permanent, high-level managerial posts in tier 1 or 2 of foreign-owned private companies.

Term Post: temporary posts for a specific period, requiring relevant qualifications, experience or expertise.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, p. 28.
Employment Pass

Three EP categories

  • Category IRM10,000 or more
  • Category IIRM5,000–RM9,999
  • Category IIIRM3,000–RM4,999

The Playbook notes revised minimum salary requirements take effect on 1 June 2026. Confirm current requirements through ESD.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, p. 28.
Professional Visit Pass

Short-term specialist work

For foreign professionals working on specific training, consultancy or project tasks for an overseas employer. The Playbook says it can last up to 12 months and is not extendable.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, pp. 28–29.

MIDA considers expertise availability, paid-up capital, salary, qualifications and related experience. Immigration obligations still apply to cross-border work.
